This Privacy Policy explains how Tamboo ("Tamboo", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Tamboo Customer app, the Tamboo Partner app, or the Tamboo website (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here.

1.2 Information collected automatically

We collect basic technical information needed to operate the Services reliably — such as device type, app version, and request logs used for debugging and fraud prevention. We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers or sell this data.

1.3 Payment information

Payments (booking tokens, advance payments, rental checkouts) are processed by Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway. Tamboo does not see or store your full card, UPI, or bank account details — Razorpay handles that directly and shares with us only what's needed to confirm a payment succeeded (amount, status, and a transaction reference).

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify it's really you via OTP
  • To let you browse halls/vendors and let venues/vendors receive and fulfil bookings and orders
  • To show real-time distance from you to a hall, when you've granted location access
  • To process payments, refunds, and — for partners — payouts of what they've earned
  • To send booking-related notifications (e.g. a reminder to a hall owner to update their booking register)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud, double-booking, or abuse of the Services

A customer and the hall/vendor they're booking with can see each other's name, contact number and booking details — this is necessary for them to coordinate an event. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Location: entirely optional — the apps work without it, you just won't see distance-to-hall. You can revoke location access any time in your device settings.
  • Camera/photos: only accessed when you choose to add a photo.
  • Notifications: can be turned off in your device settings at any time.
  • Access, correction or deletion: you can edit most of your profile directly in the app. To request a copy of your data, a correction, or full account deletion, contact us (below) — we'll action it as soon as reasonably possible, subject to any records we're legally required to keep (e.g. transaction history).

5. Data retention

We keep account and booking data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations (for example, payment records). You can request earlier deletion of your personal data as described above, where we're not legally required to retain it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the apps and our servers, and access-controlled infrastructure — to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe and will notify affected users if we ever become aware of a breach that puts your data at risk.

7. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for users who can legally enter into a booking/payment agreement in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services evolve. We'll update the "Effective date" above when we do, and for significant changes we'll make a reasonable effort to let you know in-app.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request to access, correct or delete your data? Reach us at support@tamboo.app (placeholder — replace with your real support email).
